Quick questions

Do we both need to attend?+

Ideally, yes — mortgage protection is usually set up jointly, and decisions about the cover basis and amounts are best made together. If one of you can't make it, the appointment still works and we'll follow up with the summary.

Is the appointment really free?+

Yes — completely free with no obligation. We're paid by insurers only if you choose to proceed with a recommendation, and we'll always disclose that clearly.

Can we reschedule after booking?+

Anytime, free of charge — your confirmation email contains a reschedule link. Just pick a new slot and the diary updates instantly.

Nothing suits us in the diary+

No problem — the diary only shows pre-set availability. Get in touch and we'll arrange a time manually that works for you.
