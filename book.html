Booking questions

Is the consultation really free?+

Yes — the full 45-minute review costs nothing and carries no obligation. We're paid by insurers only if you choose to proceed, which we'll always disclose.

Can I reschedule or cancel?+

Anytime, free of charge. Your confirmation email contains a reschedule link — pick a new slot and the calendar updates instantly. A courtesy reminder arrives 24 hours before.

What platform do we meet on?+

Microsoft Teams by default — the join link is in your confirmation, and it works in a browser without an account. Prefer a phone call? Note it when booking and we'll call you instead.

Who should attend the call?+

For joint mortgage protection, both partners ideally — cover basis and sum assured are joint decisions. For income protection on one person's earnings, that person alone is fine.

Can't find a time that works?+

The calendar only shows pre-set availability. If nothing fits — or you'd like something outside those windows — get in touch and we'll arrange it manually.
