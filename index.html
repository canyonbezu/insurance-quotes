What "reviewable" means

Your premium is re-priced periodically — here, compounding at 2.5% annually. Fine in year one, expensive by year ten if your health, age band or claims history shift the calculation.

What "guaranteed" means

The premium you're quoted is the premium you keep for the entire term. It can never rise due to age, claims or market conditions — your price is contractually locked.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between reviewable and guaranteed premiums?+

Guaranteed premiums are fixed for the whole term — the price never changes. Reviewable premiums are re-assessed periodically and can rise; Vitality's framework escalates at 2.5% per year, which is why the long-run totals diverge by £4,021.

Can I start on the ~£50 tier and upgrade later?+

Yes. Income protection can usually be added alongside your existing mortgage policy at any point. The one trade-off: you'll be underwritten at your age and health then, which can be slightly dearer than adding it now.

Is Aviva Global Treatment really free and automatic?+

Yes — it's bundled with qualifying Aviva protection policies at no extra premium, covers you, your partner and your children, and doesn't require a claim on your main policy to be used.

What happens to the mortgage policy if we overpay or remortgage?+

The sum assured is set against today's balance. If you clear the mortgage early you can reduce or cease cover; if you borrow more, we top up with a short add-on policy rather than replacing the whole plan.

How is the income protection benefit calculated?+

Insurers typically cap benefits at 55–65% of gross income. We target protection for your £5,594/mo household take-home, and the exact benefit is confirmed during underwriting based on Joe's earnings.
